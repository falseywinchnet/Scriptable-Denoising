MAN is Cleanup's deviation-based floor proxy; ATD is its spread statistic. Selected fractions are measured before recursive smoothing over the full context. Crossing floor uses the two nonzero samples bounding each sign change over the 0.512 s context. Its units conversion assumes white noise before registration; it is an experimental proxy, not a calibrated noise estimate. Selected fractions cover each experiment’s own evaluated frequency region: the crossing experiment covers the entire Nyquist band, while legacy Cleanup uses the receive-band setting. Fractions across those regions are not directly comparable. The variance surface also covers the full Nyquist band. Its mean threshold summarizes a varying surface; MAN/ATD are not used. The first and second passes reuse the same measured floor. Occupancy contour uses the mean of the lowest 10% of each local frequency neighborhood, tracked over time; its decision contour is low + RMS distance from low. Effective occupancy is a moment ratio, not a speech/noise probability. Shape weighting compares locally equalized, sorted observations with Cleanup’s logistic reference; it also controls peak-seed admission. The displayed shape contour is the first-pass threshold; its selected fraction is weighted seed mass. Neither score is a calibrated probability.
